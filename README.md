Turn a photo and a caption into a GTA 6-style cover: a multi-panel Vice City poster,
a neon-sunset grade, genre lettering, and a one-click PNG export,
all offline in your browser — your photos never leave your machine.
## Run it
```
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080 and start building your cover.
## Photos
Two sample photos ship in `app/samples/` so the studio works out of the box; drop in your own photos and they never leave your machine.
## Export
Pick Square, Portrait, or Wide and export all three sizes at once, or one size at a time, as PNG files.
No account, no upload, no install — just Node and a browser.
